## Method synthesis

Across the sources, the reproducible pattern is: detect before transforming; isolate one transform family per stage; keep the original immutable; verify bytecode/ABI after every stage; use at least two independent static views; use runtime/JVMTI/JNI traces for native-backed code; use emulation only as a recorded oracle; and fail closed when mappings, handlers, metadata, or behavior remain unresolved.
